HOW BASE-EAS VERIFICATION WORKS & WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Three steps from contract or docs submission to permanent Base-EAS attestation

  1. 1 · Submit primary contract(s) & docs URLProvide your Ethereum or Base vault, proxy, Safe, or timelock address plus your merged public security docs or PR.
  2. 2 · Automated dual-RPC extraction + independent verifier passPacta extracts live proxy/Safe/timelock storage across two RPCs and verifies every published governance, timelock, and role claim.

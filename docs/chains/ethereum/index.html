How the Timelock delay column picks: a protocol can have more than one timelock. The column gives the shortest delay read on any of its sampled controls, the upgrade path included, so one control with no delay makes it “none on a sampled control”. Where the upgrade path waits longer, the cell also gives the upgrade path's delay. The Upgradeability column is the upgrade path alone. “Not determined”: the published records do not settle the shortest delay.

Pacta/Privacy

Privacy

What this site collects, how conversion measurement works, and how to block it.

Summary

Pacta has no user accounts. First-party telemetry stores aggregate counts only (zero raw IP addresses). On the public production domains (pacta.wtf and pacta.fractalneurons.com), Pacta also loads the X (Twitter) conversion measurement tag (static.ads-twitter.com/uwt.js, pixel rjbqn), which sets advertising attribution cookies unless blocked by your browser or content blocker.

First-party page telemetry

Pages count anonymous, first-party aggregate page views through a small script on pacta.wtf (b.js): zero cookies, zero identifiers in browser storage, zero raw IP addresses stored, and nothing is sent by b.js when your browser sends Do Not Track (navigator.doNotTrack = "1"). A page view records only a closed-set page path, the referring site's public host name, allowlisted campaign tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) and which Pacta domain served it.

To count repeat API and MCP callers across days without storing raw IP addresses, our edge worker may compute a non-reversible keyed hash (HMAC) of a truncated network prefix and client User-Agent family over a rolling 14-day window, publishing only aggregate multi-day caller totals.

X (Twitter) Ads conversion measurement

To measure X campaign performance, public pages on pacta.wtf and pacta.fractalneurons.com load X's Universal Website Tag (https://static.ads-twitter.com/uwt.js, pixel ID rjbqn). It records ad-attributed page visits (config rjbqn), live-checker or checkout intent (tw-rjbqn-rjhie, at most once per page load, with no wallet address or form text attached), and verified self-serve orders (tw-rjbqn-rjhif, sending only the USD order value and non-personal orderId; internal QA canary transactions and idempotent replays are excluded). Staging previews and localhost never load uwt.js.

uwt.js sets cookies and sends requests to analytics.twitter.com and t.co under X's privacy policy. A small first-party localStorage entry (pacta-x-purchased) stores recent orderId strings in your browser solely so reloading a receipt never reports a duplicate purchase. To block X conversion measurement and cookies, enable tracker / third-party cookie blocking in your browser (Brave Shields, Firefox Enhanced Tracking Protection, Safari Intelligent Tracking Prevention, or uBlock Origin blocking static.ads-twitter.com, analytics.twitter.com and t.co) and review your X Ads personalization settings.

Self-serve checkout

Verify on-chain & unlock sends the product, the Base transaction hash, the target chain and address, the protocol or docs URL and, only if you enter one, a contact handle or webhook URL to Pacta's API. The contact or webhook is kept privately for delivery and never published. A payment on Base is public on-chain, like any transfer. Pay on Base talks only to the wallet in your browser; Pacta never sees your keys.

Live checks

When you press Check, the address and chain you entered are sent to Pacta's API at https://api.pacta.wtf. "Verify live on RPC" sends read-only JSON-RPC calls from your browser directly to public RPC providers (publicnode.com, drpc.org, base.org), which see your IP address under their own policies.

Watch sign-ups and pledges

When you sign up on /watch/ (or in a /scan/ result) or send a pledge there (POST /v1/requests), the protocols or contract you pick, the pledge amount and your email are sent to Pacta's API so a person at Pacta can reply from pacta@dri.network. Your email is used only for those alerts or that check, never shared, and removed on request; it is never exposed on public endpoints, sold, or used for marketing.

Requests you send

The request form sends the track, protocols, scope text and, only if you enter one, a contact handle to Pacta's API so the team can answer. Do not include personal or sensitive information beyond a contact handle. GitHub issues and emails you choose to send are handled by those services.

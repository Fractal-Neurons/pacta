What the number means

A multisig spreads one key over several holders. "3 of 5" means any three of five holders can act together. The count says how many must agree; it does not say who they are or how they decide. A single key is one holder acting alone.

The call, for Stader Labs (ETHx) on Ethereum: GET https://api.pacta.wtf/v1/check?chain=ethereum&address=0xA35b1B31Ce002FBF2058D22F30f95D405200A15b, or the MCP tool who_controls with the chain and the address. 100 calls a day are free, no key.

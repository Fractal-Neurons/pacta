About Pacta

Independent control-plane scorecards for DeFi, by Fractal Neurons.

Who is behind Pacta

Pacta is built and operated by Fractal Neurons. The code is open source under the MIT licence at github.com/Fractal-Neurons/pacta, and Pacta's onchain identity is pacta.base.eth.

What Pacta does

Pacta reads who can upgrade, pause or change 316 DeFi protocols on Ethereum and Base, and flags where that differs from their documentation. Every finding is pinned to a finalized block, can be reproduced with one cast command, and 309 attested checks are anchored on Base with EAS in the PactaCommitmentRegistry (0x35D2…d872).

Risk curators and diligence teams use it to check collateral before allocating; agents call the same checks over MCP and REST; protocol teams use it to keep their documentation and their onchain authority aligned.

Independence and funding

Pacta never sells grades. Payment never alters a Pacta classification. A status changes when onchain contract state or public documentation changes and is verified at a new finalized block — or immediately and free of charge whenever Pacta corrects an overstatement or scoping error in its own comparison. Paid verification work for protocol teams funds the engineering; the data, the API and the MCP server stay free for everyone.

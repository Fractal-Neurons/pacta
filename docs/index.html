Questions

What exactly is anchored on-chain?

Sprints 1–12 carry 12 on-chain Base EAS sprint attestations (multi-attest tx 0x8c53…001b), recorded by pacta.base.eth in PactaCommitmentRegistry. Sprints 13–14 are pinned by canonical block hash and SHA-256 summary digest in /api/v1/index.json until they are batch-attested.

How do I reproduce a record?

Every record gives the contract, selector or storage slot, block number and canonical block hash. Run the listed cast command against any archive node and compare the block hash.

What happens to exploit-sensitive findings?

Free Private Disclosure: they are never published here and are routed privately, at no cost, to official protocol security teams (security contacts, private advisories or bounty platforms).

Do I need a wallet?

No. The inspector, the radar and the JSON feed are public and read-only. Paid API access uses x402 (USDC on Base) and is optional.

How fresh is the radar?

The radar states the exact finalized block it was refreshed at (26,406,890, 2026-11-13 15:18 UTC); countdowns are computed from each operation's on-chain ETA.

2 · FROM 25 USDC

Fund a check

Hold a protocol nobody has checked independently yet? Pledge from 25 USDC toward a check of its timelocks and admin roles. Pledges for the same protocol are pooled, and we agree the scope with you by email first.

Your email is used only for this check, never shared, and removed on request.

When a check is agreed, the money goes into a two-signature Safe on Base (the founder's wallet plus an independent verifier's key; neither can move funds alone), with a limit of 500 USDC in total. Nothing is paid on this page; the Safe address is sent directly by email once the scope is agreed.

Contributions fund verification work only and never change a finding.

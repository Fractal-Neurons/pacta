1. Findings

  • owner() of the vault, accountant, teller and manager is the TimelockController 0x0DcC…df08 (getMinDelay() = 86,400s). The RolesAuthority 0xC444…99DD is also owned by it. Owner-only changes (including setManageRoot, which no role holds) therefore wait at least 24 hours.
  • Timelock roles (9 events replayed): DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE → 0x0DcC…df08; PROPOSER_ROLE → 0x0000…3f2F; EXECUTOR_ROLE → 0x0000…3f2F, 0x1D60…9312, 0x317e…A024, 0x4143…652C, 0x6Be5…526E, 0xFBf7…1A5e; CANCELLER_ROLE → 0x0000…3f2F.
  • Capabilities granted through the RolesAuthority execute with no delay. updateExchangeRate is held by the 3-of-5 Safe 0x0000…3f2F and by 0x1755…4B56 (EOA (no contract code); 8,269 transactions sent). The accountant bounds each update: upper 10004 bps, lower 10000 bps of the current rate, at least 3,600s apart (current rate 1.004425 PAXG per PAXGy; platform / performance fee 0 / 0 bps). In the upstream Veda AccountantWithRateProviders source, an update outside these bounds or sooner than the minimum delay stores the new rate and pauses the accountant instead of reverting (the deployed bytecode was not compared with that source here).
  • Full role → capability → holder map below, replayed from 68 RolesAuthority events (blocks 25,740,680–26,408,330). Selectors without a public signature are shown as raw 4-byte values.
  • Teller 0x41AB…A6f7: isPaused() = false, shareLockPeriod() = 0s. The previous teller 0x5C43…fe72 is paused (isPaused() = true).

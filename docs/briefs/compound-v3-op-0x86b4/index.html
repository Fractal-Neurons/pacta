4. Risk Notes

  • This is not a Base operation: the target is Linea's L1 MessageService (LineaRollup), and the message goes to Compound's LineaBridgeReceiver on Linea, which accepts it only from this Ethereum Timelock (govTimelock).
  • The whole reserve balance moves: Compound WETH (cWETHv3) on Linea held 25.942056261701576527 WETH of reserves at the Linea pin, exactly the 25.942056261701576527 WETH that withdrawReserves requests. Reserves only change with market activity (no borrows at the pin), so a lower balance at execution would make withdrawReserves revert on Linea.
  • Two delays stack: the 2-day Ethereum Timelock (ETA 2026-11-20 18:58:23 UTC), then the receiver queues the three Linea calls behind the 1-day Linea Timelock, executable by anyone via executeProposal within its 14-day grace period.
  • The L2 message sends 25.952056261701576527 ETH: the 25.942056261701576527 ETH unwrapped plus the 0.01 ETH fee. The Linea Timelock held 0.016655898522808438 ETH of its own at the pin, enough for the fee; with less, the third call reverts. The ETH reaches the Ethereum Timelock only after the Linea-to-Ethereum message is claimed on L1.
  • The Ethereum-side message prepays no fee (_fee = 0) and no value: delivery on Linea follows Linea's message-service claiming rules.
  • Proposal 625 was queued by CompoundGovernor (the Timelock's admin) at block 26,443,894; anyone may execute it from the ETA for the 14-day grace period. The radar reads queuedTransactions(txHash) and the Timelock's ExecuteTransaction / CancelTransaction logs.

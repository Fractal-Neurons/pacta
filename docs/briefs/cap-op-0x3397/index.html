4. Risk Notes

  • Slashing becomes two-step. Today slash() clears the operator's slashed EigenLayer shares and pays the collateral to the recipient in the same call. After the upgrade slash() only records a PendingSlash (recipient and the amount valued at slash time) and emits SlashQueued; the collateral reaches the recipient when anyone calls completeSlash(operator, slashId) after EigenLayer's slash resolution delay, and it reverts until then or while EigenLayer is paused.
  • The payout is min(amount valued at slash time, collateral this contract actually received). The source reasons that the strategy share price cannot fall before the shares are cleared, so the recipient is not short-paid; any surplus stays in the service manager.
  • Who can slash is unchanged: the slash role on this contract is held only by Cap's Delegation contract (0xF3E3Eae671000612CE3Fd15e1019154C1a4d693F) at the pin. A liquidation that slashes an EigenLayer-backed agent now delivers its collateral to the liquidator later, not in the liquidation transaction.
  • Rewards become USDC-only: distributeRewards reverts for any other token, and registerStrategy refuses a USDC collateral strategy. Delegation.distributeRewards(agent, asset) is permissionless and forwards any asset to the agent's network, so for an agent on this network it now reverts unless the asset is USDC.
  • No initializer runs (upgradeToAndCall with empty data). Storage is append-only: pendingSlashes is added at the end of the ERC-7201 EigenServiceManager storage struct, and the reentrancy guard on completeSlash uses transient storage.
  • Upgrades of this proxy are authorised only through Cap's AccessControl role bytes4(0) on this contract, whose only member at the pin is this 1-day timelock. The operation was scheduled from Cap's Developer Safe (proposer) with predecessor 0x0.

Queued timelock operations

Lista Lending — four liquidator upgrades add an emergency pause; PAUSER to a 1-of-15 Safe and the EmergencySwitchHub. One 16-call batch: upgradeToAndCall (no initializer) moves Lista's Liquidator, BrokerLiquidator, V3Liquidator and PublicLiquidator (UUPS) to implementations that add OpenZeppelin Pausable (storage layout unchanged), then grants PAUSER to a 1-of-15 Safe and the EmergencySwitchHub, and MANAGER to the hub. One signer can pause liquidations; unpause needs MANAGER.

Editorial independence

We do not publish ratings of protocols, and nobody can pay to change a result. Payment never alters a Pacta classification. A status changes when onchain contract state or public documentation changes and is verified at a new finalized block — or immediately and free of charge whenever Pacta corrects an overstatement or scoping error in its own comparison.

Editorial independence

We do not publish ratings of protocols, and nobody can pay to change a result. Payment never alters a Pacta classification. A status changes when onchain contract state or public documentation changes and is verified at a new finalized block — or immediately and free of charge whenever Pacta corrects an overstatement or scoping error in its own comparison.

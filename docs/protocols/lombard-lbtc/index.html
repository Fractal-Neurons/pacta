Queued timelock operations

Lombard — AssetRouter, BTC.b and LBTC upgrades adding a fixed deposit fee. Three ProxyAdmin.upgradeAndCall ops (no init call) upgrade AssetRouter, BTC.b (NativeLBTC) and LBTC (StakedLBTC): a per-token deposit fee is split off every proof mint to treasury (OPERATOR_ROLE may set 100-1,000 sats, the timelock anything up to half the minimum), a router-wide minRequestAmount replaces depositMinAmount, and unpauseTransfers moves from DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE to MANAGER_ROLE.

Queued timelock operations

Lombard — LBTC and BTC.b deposit fees set to 2,000 sats; minimum request doubled. One 5-call batch on the upgraded AssetRouter: minRequestAmount 2,000 -> 4,000 sats, a 2,000-sat deposit fee on every LBTC and BTC.b proof mint (half the new minimum, above the operator range), and LBTC's redeem charge moved from redeemFee (10,000 -> 0 sats) to toNativeCommission on redeems to Bitcoin (0 -> 10,000 sats).
